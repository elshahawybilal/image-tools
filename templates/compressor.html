Compress Images Online for Free

ImageKitBox helps you reduce the file size of your images directly in your browser without installing additional software.

Smaller image files can help websites load faster, reduce storage usage and make images easier to share by email or messaging apps.

How to Compress an Image

  1. Choose a JPG, PNG or WEBP image from your device.
  2. Select the compression quality you want.
  3. Click the Compress Image button.
  4. Download the compressed image to your device.

Supported Image Formats

The ImageKitBox Image Compressor currently supports JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP images.

The maximum upload size is 20 MB per image.

Image Quality and File Size

Higher quality settings usually produce larger files while lower quality settings can reduce the file size more aggressively.

For most photos, the High Quality setting provides a good balance between image quality and file size.

Why Compress Images?

Large images can slow down websites, use more bandwidth and take longer to upload or download.

Compressing images can help improve website loading speed and reduce the amount of storage or data required for each image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Image Compressor free?

Yes. You can use the ImageKitBox Image Compressor online for free.

Which image formats can I compress?

The compressor currently supports JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP images.

Will compression reduce image quality?

JPG and WEBP compression may reduce some image quality depending on the selected setting. Higher quality settings preserve more detail.

PNG images are optimized using lossless compression whenever possible.

What is the maximum upload size?

The current maximum upload size is 20 MB per image.

Do I need to install software?

No. The compressor works directly from your web browser.
