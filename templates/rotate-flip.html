How to Rotate or Flip an Image

  1. Choose an image from your device.
  2. Select a rotate or flip option.
  3. Click Apply Changes .
  4. Download the edited image.

Why Rotate or Flip Images?

Rotating an image can fix its orientation, while flipping can create a mirrored version or reverse the image direction.
