Resize Images Online for Free

ImageKitBox makes it easy to resize an image to the exact dimensions you need. Choose an image from your device, enter the new width and height in pixels and download the resized image.

You can use the image resizer for common image formats such as JPG, PNG and WebP without installing additional image editing software.

How to Resize an Image

Resizing an image only takes a few simple steps.

  1. Choose an image from your device.
  2. Enter the new width in pixels.
  3. Enter the new height in pixels.
  4. Click the Resize Image button.
  5. Download the resized image to your device.

Resize Images to Exact Dimensions

Enter the exact pixel dimensions you need for your image. The width and height can each be set from 1 pixel up to 10000 pixels.

This can be useful when you need specific image dimensions for websites, documents, social media or other digital projects.

Why Use ImageKitBox Image Resizer?

The ImageKitBox image resizer is designed to provide a quick and simple way to change image dimensions online. There is no need to install additional software. Upload your image, enter the dimensions you need and resize it directly through your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. You can use the ImageKitBox image resizer online for free.

You can enter a width and height between 1 and 10000 pixels.
