Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ImageKitBox handles information when you use our website and online image tools.

Images and Files

Images uploaded to ImageKitBox are used to perform the image processing operation you request, such as converting or resizing an image.

Uploaded images are processed for the requested operation and are not intended to be permanently stored as part of the normal use of our image tools.

Information We May Collect

We may collect basic technical and usage information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, approximate location, IP address and interactions with the website.

This information may be used to understand website usage, improve our tools and website performance, and help maintain the security and reliability of the service.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

ImageKitBox may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand how visitors use the website and provide analytics or advertising services.

The use of certain cookies may depend on your location and applicable consent requirements.

Google Analytics

ImageKitBox uses Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors use the website.

Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, interactions with the website, device and browser information, approximate location and general usage statistics.

We use this information to understand website traffic and improve our tools and user experience.

Advertising

ImageKitBox may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services in the future.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalize advertising according to their own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements.

Third-Party Services

ImageKitBox may use third-party services for analytics, hosting, advertising or other functionality required to operate and improve the website.

These third-party services may process certain information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to operate ImageKitBox securely and protect the website and its services. However, no method of transmitting or processing data over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, tools, third-party services or applicable requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how ImageKitBox handles information, you can contact us through our Contact page .
